NFPA 25 § 15.6 — Restoration Notification Required

Per NFPA 25, once an impaired system is restored to full service, the Impairment Coordinator must notify all previously contacted parties and confirm the system is back in service. Use this form to close out your impairment with National Safety & Risk.
