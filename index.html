NFPA 25 — Impairment Notification Requirement

Per NFPA 25 (Standard for the Inspection, Testing, and Maintenance of Water-Based Fire Protection Systems), all sprinkler system impairments — planned or emergency — must be reported to the property owner/occupant, the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ), and the monitoring company prior to or immediately upon the start of impairment. This form initiates the required notification process with National Safety & Risk.

NFPA 25 § 15.4 — Impairment Coordinator

NFPA 25 requires the property owner or designated representative to assign an Impairment Coordinator responsible for notifying the fire department, insurance carrier, alarm company, and building occupants.
