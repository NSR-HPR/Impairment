Impairment Notification Requirement

All sprinkler system impairments — planned or emergency — must be reported to the property owner/occupant, the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ), and the monitoring company prior to or immediately upon the start of impairment. This form initiates the required notification process with National Safety & Risk.
